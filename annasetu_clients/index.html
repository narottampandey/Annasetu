Platform Features

Food Donation

Houses, hostels, mess halls and marriage venues can donate surplus food easily.

Human Support

NGOs and individuals can collect food for people in need across the community.

Cattle Feed

Food not suitable for humans is repurposed as nutritious cattle feed.

Organic Fertilizer

Food waste is transformed into organic fertilizer for sustainable farming.

AI Classification

AI automatically categorizes food quality for the most appropriate usage.

About Annasetu

Annasetu ("Anna" = food, "Setu" = bridge) is a platform dedicated to reducing food waste by bridging the gap between food surplus and food need. Every meal saved is a step toward a more equitable and sustainable world.
